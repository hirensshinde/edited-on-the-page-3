About me

From classroom computer science to shipped mobile apps.

I'm Vishal Prajapati, a mobile application developer based in Ahmedabad, India. I currently work as an Android developer at Veyon Labs, where I've been building and maintaining native and cross-platform apps since 2024.

My formal background is in computer applications — I completed my Master of Computer Applications (MCA) from SSIT, Gandhinagar, in 2026. That foundation in algorithms, databases and software architecture shapes how I approach every app I build: start with the data model, keep the UI honest, and never ship something I haven't tested on a real device.

I work primarily across Android Studio, Flutter and Xcode, which lets me take a project from a native Android build to a cross-platform Flutter release, or package it for iOS when a client needs it. I enjoy the full cycle — architecture, UI, local storage, and the final polish that makes an app feel trustworthy in someone's pocket.

My most recent personal project, Groot, is an income and expense tracker built to be fast enough to use in the middle of a conversation — log a transaction in two taps, see your balance update instantly, no sign-up required.
